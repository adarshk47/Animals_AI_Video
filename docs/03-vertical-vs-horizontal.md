# Vertical (9:16) vs Horizontal (16:9)

Decide this in the scenario, before generating any image. Switching later means regenerating everything.

| | Vertical 9:16 | Horizontal 16:9 |
|--|---------------|-----------------|
| Use for | YouTube Shorts, Reels, TikTok | YouTube videos |
| Length | 30-60 seconds | 2-5 minutes |
| Shots | about 6-10 | about 25-40 |
| Pace | fast, a hook in the first 2 seconds | slower, story builds |
| Framing | one animal per frame, close-ups | wide shots, two animals side by side possible |
| Dialogue | short lines (under 10 words) | longer exchanges |
| Subtitles | big, centred, always on | smaller, bottom |
| Resolution | 1080 x 1920 | 1920 x 1080 |

## Tips for vertical
- Open with the funniest or most striking image.
- Keep the animal's face in the centre; leave the bottom 20% free for subtitles and app buttons.
- Cut every 3-5 seconds.

## Tips for horizontal
- Start with an establishing shot of the jungle or city, then cut to the characters.
- Use wide shots with two animals when it works; otherwise shot / reverse shot.
- Vary the scenes so a 3-minute video does not look repetitive.

## Making both from one story
Write the story once. For vertical, trim to the best 4-6 lines. For horizontal, keep the full dialogue. Regenerate the images in the new aspect ratio rather than cropping.
