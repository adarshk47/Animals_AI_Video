# Character Bible

One entry per animal. Keep the **look prompt** word-for-word identical every time you generate that animal. Save the first good image as the animal's reference image and reuse it in every shot.

Voice settings are descriptions. Pick a matching voice in your text-to-speech tool, then write the chosen voice name in the "Voice chosen" line so you reuse it in every video.

---

## Tips
- Keep one distinctive mark per animal (the lion's scar, the dog's red collar). It helps you spot an inconsistent shot.
- If you want a cartoon style instead of photorealistic, replace `photorealistic` with your style word in every look prompt, and do it for all animals at once.
