# Background Library

Paste one of these into the **background** field of a prompt card. Change the background by swapping one line. Each has a day and a night variant: replace the lighting phrase.

- Day lighting: `bright natural daylight`
- Night lighting: `moonlight, deep blue shadows, soft glow`

## Jungle

| Name | Prompt |
|------|--------|
| Dense jungle | `dense tropical jungle, tall trees, thick green leaves, hanging vines, mist, shafts of sunlight, {lighting}` |
| River | `jungle river with calm water, mossy rocks, overhanging trees, {lighting}` |
| Clearing | `open grassy clearing in a jungle, tall trees around, wildflowers, {lighting}` |
| Big tree | `giant old banyan tree with thick roots and branches, jungle behind, {lighting}` |
| Waterfall | `jungle waterfall splashing into a pool, wet rocks, green moss, {lighting}` |

## City

| Park | `city park with benches, trees, lamp posts, walking path, buildings in the distance, {lighting}` |
| Market | `crowded street market with fruit stalls and colourful awnings, {lighting}` |
| Night city | `city street at night with neon signs, wet road reflections, street lamps, quiet` |
| Balcony | `apartment balcony with railing and potted plants, city view, {lighting}` |

## How to use
1. Pick a name, copy the prompt, and replace `{lighting}` with the day or night phrase.
2. Use the same background prompt for every shot in one scene so it looks like one place.
3. Add a new background by adding a row in the right table.

## Mixing jungle and city
For stories where an animal moves between the two, make them separate scenes with a cut, e.g. jungle clearing, then busy street. Keep the animal's reference image the same.
