# Sound Effects List

Add these in the editor (step 7). Layer 1-2 effects per shot at low volume; animal voices stay clearest.

## Animals
| Animal | Effects |
|--------|---------|
| Lion | roar, low growl, soft purr/breathing, footsteps on grass |
| Monkey | chatter, screech, laugh-like hoots, leaves rustling when climbing |
| Dog | bark, whine, panting, paws on pavement |
| Chimpanzee | hoots, pant-grunts, knuckle steps |
| Pigeon | wing flaps, cooing |

## Jungle ambience
birds calling, insects, distant monkeys, running water, wind in leaves, rain on leaves

## City ambience
traffic, honking, scooters, crowd murmur, shop bells, construction, night city hum

## Where to get them
- Free libraries with commercial-use sounds (check each file's licence): Pixabay, Freesound (filter by licence), YouTube Audio Library.
- Built into editors like CapCut and DaVinci Resolve.
- AI sound-effect generators exist; use them if you need something specific.

Always check the licence before publishing, especially for monetised videos.
