# Example: "The Missing Mango" (jungle, vertical 9:16, English, about 40s)

## Scenario
- **Language:** English
- **Format:** Vertical 9:16, about 40 seconds
- **Setting:** Jungle, day (Big tree, then Clearing)
- **Mood:** funny

| Animal | Name | Role |
|--------|------|------|
| Lion | Sher | Accuses the monkey of stealing his mango |
| Monkey | Bandar | The thief, who denies it |

**Story:** Sher the lion finds his mango missing. Bandar, up the tree, swears he knows nothing, but he is holding the mango behind his back. Sher roars, Bandar drops the mango and runs.

## Dialogue
```
SHER: Who took my mango?
BANDAR: Not me! I was sleeping!
SHER: Then why are you holding it?
BANDAR: Holding what? Oh... this? (laughs)
(Sher roars. Bandar runs up the tree)
```
